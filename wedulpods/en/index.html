WedulPods · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 27, 2026 · 한국어

WedulPods (package ID com.wedul.wedulpods) is an Android app that receives Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) broadcasts from nearby compatible earbuds and displays battery status in the app, home-screen widget, and notifications.

The app has no account or developer-operated server and does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer. The current release has no ads or in-app purchases.

1. Information processed and purpose

  • Nearby BLE signals are processed while the app is running to display compatible earbuds' battery, charging, and wear status and provide optional features. The app does not connect to or send commands to earbuds.
  • App preferences, such as auto-pause, case-open detection, widget color, and onboarding state, are stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Received earbud signals are used to update the app, widget, and notifications. The app does not store them in an account or developer server. Android may retain rendered widget or notification content to display it.

The app cannot determine ownership of nearby compatible earbuds. It does not identify or store Bluetooth addresses.

2. Permissions

  • Nearby-device scanning is used to receive BLE advertisements. The app does not collect location or infer location from Bluetooth signals.
  • Notification access (optional feature) is used only when you enable auto-pause, to check active media playback state and request playback control. The app does not read or store notification titles, contents, or source-app information. Battery display and other features remain available if you decline this permission.
  • Notifications are used to show battery status and low-battery alerts on your device.
  • Foreground service and boot completed support BLE reception while monitoring is enabled and restore monitoring after a device restart.

The app does not declare the Internet, location, or advertising ID permissions. It does not request contacts, call logs, messages, camera, or microphone access.

3. Retention and deletion

Preferences remain in the app's private storage while you use the app. Clear the app's data in Android Settings or uninstall the app to remove them. Earbud signals are not transmitted to a developer server or retained in an account.

4. Sharing and service providers

The developer does not sell or share personal data collected through the app. The current app does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. Google Play and the Android operating system may process information under their own policies to provide installation, security, and device services.

5. Children's privacy

This app is not designed for children. It does not collect users' personal data to a developer-operated server.

6. Your choices and rights

You can change Bluetooth and notification permissions in your device settings. Turning off notification access disables only auto-pause; other app features remain available. Contact the developer for privacy-related questions or requests.

7. Security

The app has no developer-operated server, user account, or network transmission feature and does not declare Internet permission. Preferences are kept in Android's app-private storage. Security of the operating system, device backups, rooting, and other conditions outside the app depends on your device and platform settings.

8. Future changes

The current release has no ads or in-app purchases. If payment features, advertising, or new data processing are added, this policy and relevant Google Play disclosures will be updated before that version is released.

9. Trademark notice

WedulPods is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Apple Inc. Apple, AirPods, and Beats are trademarks of their respective owners and are mentioned only to describe compatibility.
